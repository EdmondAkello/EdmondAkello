🧭 About Me
I'm a Civil Engineer working at the intersection of engineering hydrology, hydraulic design, road infrastructure, GIS, and software development.
I build open-source tools that turn engineering data and established analytical methods into practical, repeatable workflows. My interests span the full path from road-design data and digital terrain models to catchment analysis, drainage investigations, and structured engineering outputs.
My approach is to make engineering computation more transparent, reproducible, testable, and useful in real projects—without losing sight of assumptions, coordinate systems, or the engineering meaning of the results.

🧠 Development Principles
🎯 Engineering-backed: methods, assumptions, and limitations should be explicit.
🔍 Traceable: inputs and outputs should be understandable and reviewable.
🧪 Testable: computational workflows should be checked against known or independently verified results.
🔄 Reproducible: repeatable processing is preferable to undocumented manual steps.
🔗 Interoperable: engineering and GIS data should move between tools with clear spatial-reference handling.
🌍 Practical: software should address real infrastructure and environmental problems.
